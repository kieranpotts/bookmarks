= Voice and speech tools

Applications for working with text by voice, including voice-to-text dictation,
text-to-speech readers, and text-to-speech engines.

== Voice-to-text dictation

* https://wisprflow.ai/[Wispr Flow] – Voice-to-text AI that turns speech into clear, polished writing in any application, across Mac, Windows, iOS, and Android.

== Text-to-speech readers

* https://speechify.com/[Speechify] – Text-to-speech and Voice AI assistant that reads documents aloud, transcribes speech to text, and creates podcasts across web, desktop, and mobile.
* https://www.naturalreaders.com/[NaturalReader] – AI-powered text-to-speech for personal reading, commercial voiceovers, and education, with content-aware multilingual voices and voice cloning.

== Text-to-speech engines

* https://github.com/OHF-Voice/piper1-gpl[Piper] – A fast, local neural text-to-speech engine that embeds espeak-ng for phonemization. Runs offline via CLI, web server, Python, or C/C++ APIs, and is used by Home Assistant, NVDA, and others.
* https://pied.mikeasoft.com/[Pied] – A graphical installer and manager for Piper neural TTS voices, integrating Piper with Speech Dispatcher for use with screen readers such as Orca.
